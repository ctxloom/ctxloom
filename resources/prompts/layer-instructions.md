These tools retrieve context (fragments, commands, prior sessions) and delegate: agent_run launches a configured ctxloom agent as a child session; agent_send reaches only your parent ("parent") and your own children; mail sent to you triggers your next turn and arrives there as context, so there is nothing to poll or wait on. Manage ctxloom (bundles, profiles, remotes, review, trust, hooks) with the ctxloom CLI in your shell, not tools here. Tasks live in the separate `taskloom` MCP server and CLI.
